About

I'm an aspiring electrical engineer with a growing interest in semiconductor technologies. I'm currently taking Microelectronic Technologies, and I'm especially interested in semiconductor physics: how doping, temperature and applied voltage reshape the energy bands, and how that turns into the behavior of real devices.

I built Fermi’s Sandbox to make those ideas something you can play with instead of only read about. Pick a material, change the doping, form a pn junction and bias it, and watch the bands, fields and currents respond.

Connect on LinkedIn

Models: nondegenerate statistics, complete ionization, abrupt (step) junction with the depletion approximation, and the ideal diode equation (no generation or recombination inside the depletion region). Notation and Si, Ge and GaAs parameters follow R. F. Pierret, Semiconductor Device Fundamentals (Ch. 2, 3, 5 and 6). In the animation, carrier energies are spread out and particle rates compressed so the behavior is visible; every number on the page is computed from the full equations.
